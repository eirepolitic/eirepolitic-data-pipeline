# Campaign brief — Constitutional change close vote

## Goal

Explain one of the closest recorded Dáil divisions in the current EirePolitic 2026 voting snapshot: the 8 July 2026 Second Stage vote on the Planning for Constitutional Change Bill 2026.

## Audience

General Irish political audience. Assume no prior knowledge of Bill stages or the exact proposal.

## Core fact

The Bill was defeated at Second Stage by **69 Tá to 79 Níl**, a margin of **10 votes**.

## Core editorial distinction

This was **not** a direct vote on Irish unity. It was a vote on whether this particular statutory planning process should proceed: a Government Green Paper, consultation, an all-island Citizens' Assembly and reporting to the Oireachtas.

## Editorial objective

Make the vote understandable in under 30 seconds while preserving the difference between:

- support/opposition to a particular planning mechanism and timetable; and
- support/opposition to Irish reunification itself.

## Recommended format

Six-slide Instagram carousel.

## Status

Research certified for editorial use. Party-level vote totals are deliberately excluded until event-date party attribution is complete enough for publication.
