What did Fianna Fáil TDs speak about most often in the Dáil?

Across 8,421 classified speech records from current Fianna Fáil TDs, the most frequent issue category was Housing and Community Development, followed by Health and Education.

Coverage: 18 Dec 2024–26 Feb 2026. Counts show classified speech frequency, not party policy, priority or position.

Source: Houses of the Oireachtas data, classified by Eirepolitic.
