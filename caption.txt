Bills of the Current Session — Enacted · Post 1.

This carousel looks at three Bills enacted during the current parliamentary session: the Strategic Gas Reserve Bill, the Israeli Settlements goods Bill and the Criminal Law, Civil Law and Defence Bill.

Where a recorded Dáil division is shown, the chart describes that exact proposition. In particular, the Israeli Settlements vote shown here was on a services amendment, not a final vote on the whole Bill.

Sources: Houses of the Oireachtas records, EirePolitic’s date-correct party-history reconstruction for recorded divisions, and relevant official departmental material.
