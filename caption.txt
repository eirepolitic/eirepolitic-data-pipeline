Bills of the Current Session · First Stage · Post 1

This post explains three Bills currently listed at First Stage and what that stage means in the Irish legislative process.

• Protection of Tenants' Deposits Bill 2025
• Energy Poverty Reduction (Use of Surplus Renewable Energy) Bill 2025
• Criminal Justice (Trespass on Land) Bill 2025

First Stage is the formal initiation step. It does not by itself show that the House supports the Bill’s general principles.
